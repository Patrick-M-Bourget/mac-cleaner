## Limits

Small reason/retention text remains visually readable at original dimensions; no enlarged-image or contrast-ratio claim is made. Gallery actions are inert, so this review establishes presentation only, not behavior, keyboard access, persistence or actual unavailable-store recovery. Live journey evidence is separate. Only Dark at these two fixed sizes is covered here; Light is assigned separately. No offscreen content is inferred.
