What’s new in 1.0.1

Empty the Trash from CacheDrop, get a weekly reminder to clean up, and run CacheDrop on more Macs.

  • Empty Trash… lists everything in the Trash before it deletes it, from Overview and the menu bar.
  • The menu bar menu shows what the Trash holds, with Open Trash.
  • Settings can remind you weekly to clean up when a scan finds at least 1 GB ready to clean. It’s off until you turn it on.
  • CacheDrop checks for updates once a week. Nothing is downloaded or installed until you choose, and Settings can turn it off.
  • CacheDrop now runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and later, on Intel Macs too.
  • After an uninstall, CacheDrop rescans only what changed, and Results says the Trash amount once.
