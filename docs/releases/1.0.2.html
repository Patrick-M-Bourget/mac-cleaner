What’s new in 1.0.2

Audio software gets its own place in Applications, Explore storage can show a storage map, and you can clean up from Terminal.

  • Applications has an Audio software section: DAWs and apps, plug-ins by maker, sounds and samples, and drivers.
  • Sounds and samples open their maker’s app when it manages them, such as Waves Central, and drivers and Reason’s additional devices can go to the Trash.
  • Explore storage has a Map view and its own place in the sidebar, under Storage.
  • When an open app holds items, the review names it once, above the groups, with a way to quit it.
  • Clean up from Terminal with cachedrop clean, and add --dry-run to see what it would do first.
  • Help › What’s New in CacheDrop shows these notes for the version you’re running.
