What’s new in 1.0.0

Reviews open at once, results lead with the space you got back, and pages are ready sooner after a cleanup.

  • Opened from the disk image, CacheDrop offers to move itself to your Applications folder.
  • The review sheet opens as soon as you click Clean up, with one line for each app holding items.
  • Results lead with one number: the space you measurably got back.
  • After a cleanup, CacheDrop rescans only what changed, and Applications no longer waits for it.
  • History shows what each cleanup freed, and Overview says Empty when nothing is recommended.
  • Clearer, calmer wording across the app, and the review sheet fits what it lists.
