What’s new in 0.9.4

First use is clearer, the window is calmer, and CacheDrop can now check for updates itself.

  • Check for Updates… in the CacheDrop menu and in Settings finds and installs new versions, and waits for a running cleanup to finish first.
  • First use guides you through access and getting your results ready, and you can cancel it at any time.
  • System access has its own page in the sidebar, apart from Locations.
  • Applications shows what your selection moves to the Trash, and Smart clean and the Cleanup plan sort by name or size.
  • One sidebar look in light and dark mode, a softer page edge, and a ghost for files from deleted apps.
  • Results name each Xcode project, and Locations says why a folder wasn’t read in full.
