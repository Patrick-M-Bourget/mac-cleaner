What’s new in 1.0.4

A simpler first run, Smart clean on one screen, and suggestions worth a look.

  • Welcome shows what CacheDrop cleans and what it never touches. The first scan shows each category as it finishes, then leads straight to Smart clean.
  • Smart clean fits on one screen: what it frees, the categories it cleans, and one Clean up button.
  • Worth a look suggests apps you haven’t opened in 6 months and files left by deleted apps, never ticked for you.
  • After a cleanup moves items to the Trash, Results offers Empty from Trash first, so the space is really free.
  • The Full Disk Access sheet stays open after you grant access and says it’s done.
  • Applications, Locations and Settings are simpler, and wording is the same everywhere: Scan again, dry run and the Trash.
