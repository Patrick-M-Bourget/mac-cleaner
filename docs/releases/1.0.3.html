What’s new in 1.0.3

Photo and video gets its own category, with Adobe and Final Cut Pro, and you can choose to share how far setup got.

  • Photo and video is a new category for Adobe’s media cache, Premiere Pro’s and After Effects’ caches, and Final Cut Pro.
  • An Adobe media cache you moved in Adobe’s settings is found too, along with its Peak Files.
  • Final Cut Pro’s render files are part of Smart clean. Optimized and proxy media are listed per library, for you to choose one by one.
  • Welcome and Settings have a Share setup progress option, off unless you turn it on. It shares when you first open, scan and clean up, never what’s on this Mac.
  • cachedrop clean tells you about your trial or license before it asks to clean up.
  • Links under a row’s name in Applications are now the same size as the row’s other links.
